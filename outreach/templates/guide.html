Setup guide

One-time setup to connect your Microsoft email IDs (about 15 minutes)

1 Register the app in Microsoft (one time)

This gives the tool permission to send and read email for the mailboxes you sign in with. Do it with your Microsoft 365 admin account.

  1. Open entra.microsoft.com and sign in with your admin account.
  2. In the left menu go to Identity → Applications → App registrations (or search "App registrations" at the top).
  3. Click + New registration.
    • Name: My Outreach Tool (anything you like)
    • Supported account types: "Accounts in this organizational directory only (Single tenant)"
    • Redirect URI: choose platform Web and paste:
    Click Register.
  4. You're now on the app's Overview page. Copy the Application (client) ID and the Directory (tenant) ID. You'll paste them in Settings.

2 Add the email permissions

  1. In the app's left menu click API permissions → + Add a permission → Microsoft Graph → Delegated permissions.
  2. Search and tick: Mail.Send, Mail.ReadWrite, offline_access (User.Read is already there).
  3. Click Add permissions, then Grant admin consent for (your company) and confirm. All rows should show a green tick.

3 Create a client secret

  1. Left menu: Certificates & secrets → Client secrets → + New client secret.
  2. Description: outreach. Expires: 24 months. Click Add.
  3. Copy the Value right away (not the "Secret ID"). Microsoft only shows it once.

4 Paste the details into Settings

Go to Settings and paste the Client ID, Tenant ID and Client secret value. Choose your time zone and click Save.

5 Connect your email IDs

  1. Go to Mailboxes and click Connect Microsoft mailbox.
  2. Sign in with the first email ID and accept the permissions.
  3. Repeat for every email ID: click Connect again and choose "Use another account".
  4. Set each mailbox's daily limit (e.g. 25) and signature, and send yourself a test email.
Each email ID must be a real (licensed) mailbox you can sign in to. If an email ID is only an alias, it can't sign in. Use its main mailbox instead.

6 Run your first campaign

  1. Leads: upload your CSV and give the list a name.
  2. Campaigns: create a campaign, write email 1 and follow-ups, and click Preview to check them.
  3. Leads tab: add your list. Schedule & settings tab: pick mailboxes, your leads' time zone and sending hours.
  4. Click Start campaign. Keep this window (and your PC) on during sending hours. If the PC is off, sending simply continues next time.

✓ Deliverability checklist

  • Each domain has SPF, DKIM and DMARC set up in Microsoft 365 (Admin center → Settings → Domains, and Defender → Email authentication → DKIM).
  • Keep each mailbox at 20–30 emails/day, including follow-ups.
  • Verify your lead list before uploading. Keep bounces under 2–3%. The tool auto-pauses a campaign above your limit.
  • Plain-text style, short emails, max one link, no attachments or images.
  • Watch the Activity log for errors. If Microsoft blocks a mailbox, it's paused automatically.
